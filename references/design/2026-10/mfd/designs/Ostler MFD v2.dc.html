Pages, not modesEvery gauge and view is a page that can sit in any tile. Saved layouts (Trail, Convoy, Commute, Workshop) replace drive modes and sync per car and screen.
Shape, not deviceWide tiles put parts side by side, tall tiles stack them, small tiles show one number, strips show a chip. Try the layout buttons.
While movingSaved layouts and page swaps still work (one tap, like a bezel key). Changing the layout is locked. Logs records but holds charts until parked.
Caution and warningAmber caution rings the source tile and lights the strip. Red warning takes the passenger-most tile that isn't a car instrument, until ACK.
