OSTLER GATEWAY · THE ONE APP THAT TALKS TO THE CAR
Gateway: connections, cars, permissions
The gateway owns the hardware and stores everything: logs, history, car profiles, settings, alerts. Every other app is a viewer and control on top, and only talks to the gateway. Its own navigation: Status · Cars · Apps · This device. Settings are in three levels: account (synced), car profile (one per car), and this device.
