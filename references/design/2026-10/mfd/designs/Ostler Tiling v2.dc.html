OSTLER · TILING · V2
When the whole window gets squeezed
The same live MFD shell, given different windows by Android split screen, a smaller head unit or a phone. Two rules on top of the page sizes: a tall window turns splits vertical and a short one lays tiles in a row; if tiles would still be too small, the main page keeps the space and the rest become chips. Each tile label shows its size. All screens are live: tap a label to swap a page.
1 · ShapeWindow taller than wide: splits turn vertical. Shorter than 320 px of tile space: one row.
2 · SizeEach tile picks Chip, Compact, Standard or Expanded from its own space.
3 · OverflowStill too small: main page plus chips. Nothing is ever squeezed below the minimum type size.
