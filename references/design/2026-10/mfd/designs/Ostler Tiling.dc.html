Every screen can show any app, side by side
Each Ostler app still installs and runs on its own. When several are installed, a screen splits into fixed tiles, like a cockpit display: each tile shows one page from any app, tiles opened from each other stay linked, and layouts are saved per car and screen. Night language as in the Ostler app.
