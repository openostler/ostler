Drag anywhere: drop zones and suggested layouts
A revision of E5 and E6, frames Z-01 to Z-09. Night and LHD unless tagged. Drop zones on 7in and up, a Hyprland-style half rule on 5in and phones, and Snap-style suggested layouts on 10in and tablets. The holo shows targets only; the reflowed tiles are the preview.
ZonesFour triangles along the diagonals split on that side; the centre swaps (moving a tile) or replaces (from the tray). Each zone is at least 64 px.
Only valid zonesA zone appears only if the result keeps both pages inside their size ranges. Compact tiles keep the centre and the longer-side split.
Preview is the truthThe holo is faint and only marks targets. Hovering a zone reflows the real tiles; nothing is a ghost.
By screen5in and phone: half rule, no holo. 7in: zones. 10in and tablet: zones plus suggested layouts at the strip.
