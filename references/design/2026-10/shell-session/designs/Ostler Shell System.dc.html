Ostler shell: pages, tiles and editing, together
Everything agreed so far, in 34 frames, with no top strip: one page on small windows, two on extra-large ones; Android-style edit mode with tiling on top; stacks and full screen; two-level focus. Sizes in dp. Night, LHD. Detail lives in the section files (E, F, V, L, W).
PagesA page is a column at least 720 dp wide. Small windows show one page; extra-large ones show two (three on ultra-wide), plus a pinned driver column from 12″ up. Shrinking the window spills pages back into the carousel.
AlertsNever modal. The app writes the message and styles its own content; the tiler only routes it. Card centred on arrival, then a readable tab in the passenger-side top corner that stays until it clears. On screen: ring plus a tag. Bordered dots and badges show where. Spoken once; warnings tone until ACK.
Page dotsAlways at the bottom, and only in edit mode or while the carousel moves; they fade after about 1.5 s. Stacks have no page limit: their badges come in pages, with their own dots that show only while turning or editing.
Android firstHold to edit: pages zoom out on translucent panels with a selector. Tap a tile for its resize frame. Drag to the edge to change page; past the last, a new page appears.
Then tilingWhile dragging: a shapes row at the top (2+1, 1+2, Halves, Quad, More), zones on tiles (slide to swap, hold the centre to stack, edges split), a delete circle at the bottom.
Two versionsSpanning tiles and wide layouts keep a Wide and a Single-page version; neither overwrites the other.
SafetyNo editing while moving. Stacks, full screen, layouts and the wheel all still work. Warnings go to the driver side on big screens.
