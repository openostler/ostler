Edit mode the Android way: zoom out, see your pages
Frames W-01 to W-05. Night, LHD, parked. Replaces the “Move to page 2” prompt and the edge glow with Android’s zoomed-out pages.
Copy Android exactlyHold: pages zoom out to about 82% without reflowing, each on its own translucent panel; the selector outlines the page you’re working on. Drag to the edge to scroll and the selector follows; past the last page, an empty one appears.
Then add tilingOn the page you’re over: tiles slide aside (swap), hold the centre to stack, edges to split, the shapes row at the top, the delete circle at the bottom.
Shell vs appWindow style (frame, transparency, corners) is the shell’s, set by holding a selected tile. Page content settings belong to the app.
