Edit mode v3: hold, drag, stack
Frames V-01 to V-11, S-01 to S-07 and P-01 to P-03. Night and LHD unless tagged. Supersedes the − buttons, Add page and the per-tile dropdown in sections E and F.
Android firstHold to edit, tap a tile for its resize frame, drag any edge. Page dots at the bottom; hold near the edge to turn. Then the tiling: shapes row, drop zones, stacks.
One gestureDrag a tile: up to a layout slot, onto tiles (slide to swap, hold the centre to stack, edges to split), down to remove, to the side to turn the carousel.
No tile dropdownsThe label is just the name. Changing a page, closing and stacking all happen in edit mode; page settings live in each app’s own menu.
BadgesA stack’s badges, and full-screen badges, mean “switch here”. One tap, safe while moving. Edge: by bezel keys if any, else passenger side; bottom in portrait. Customisable.
Two-level focusTile level, then OK into the page. Arrows never leave a page at its edge; Back does.
