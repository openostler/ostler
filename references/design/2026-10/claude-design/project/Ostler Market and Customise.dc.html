Make Ostler yours — safely
Add-ons work like a store with Installed and Available tabs, but have no ratings, ranks or ads. Every item can be renamed, re-iconed, moved and resized. Safety items can move but never be removed. While Moving, both the market and edit mode are locked. Integrations and “Runs on the Brain” are marked provisional until the HA research round lands.
