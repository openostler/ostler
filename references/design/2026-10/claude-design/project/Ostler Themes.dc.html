OSTLER · THEME ENGINE · EXPLORATION
Eight design languages, five core screens
Every theme is a token file: palette, accent, radius, type, glow, transparency, texture, gauge style and map filter. Status colours, the fault telltale, the Moving limits and minimum sizes stay locked. Each theme gets an automatic Moving-safe variant that drops blur, glow and texture and makes cards opaque. HU-7 Drive frames show that variant, and a Parked frame follows wherever the two differ.
dark_modeBEST ON HEAD UNITS AT NIGHTDeep night (least light), Night, Heritage and Race. Heritage loses its texture and Race its glow when Moving. Expedition works day and night.
light_modeDAYTIME / SUNLIGHTHigh contrast is the sunlight and accessibility theme. It is too bright for night cabins, so Auto should hand over to Night after dusk.
phone_iphonePARKED / PHONE FIRSTGlass only shows its full look parked or on phone and tablet; head units get the opaque variant. Minimal is fine on head units, but its thin type needs the 500-weight Moving lift.
R4Round 4 · originals rebuilt
Night: kept as the baseline, now with a floating strip, pill chips and 20 px cards.
Heritage: oxblood leather, walnut grain, brass hairline inlay, DM Serif headings, engraved small-caps labels, needle dials and a folder-tab rail.
Expedition: olive frames, topo-contour background, stencilled sand tags, condensed uppercase, 2–4 px corners, thick bars and a map-first layout.
Glass: aqua bokeh, leaf-cornered cards (24/24/24/6), floating dock and capsule strip.
Minimal: no card shapes, only hairline underlines, Manrope 200 at 40 px, 24 px gaps and bare digits.
Race: carbon weave, a yellow leading edge on every card, asymmetric corners, italic condensed numbers, a chamfered hero and the shift-light bar.
Deep night: true black, dimmed text and accent, near-invisible hairlines, bare digits, and a violet ramp held to its darkest steps.
High contrast: white, a 3 px black stroke on everything, heavy full-ink labels, thick segmented gauges and a squared hero.
R3Round 3 · design-language packs
Slab · neo-brutalism: 3 px black outlines, hard 5 px offset shadows, pastel lilac/cyan on cream, dot grid.
Prism · glassmorphism: vivid bokeh behind 12 % frosted panels with bright glass rims.
Paper · flat minimalism: white, black type, no decoration, greyscale ramp.
Soft · neumorphism: every surface extruded from one grey with paired light and dark shadows.
Bakelite · skeuomorphism: moulded brown plastic, chrome-bezel keys, VFD digits, needle dials.
Clay · claymorphism: 32 px puffy pastel shapes with inner highlights and inflated shadows.
Tonal · in the spirit of Material: tonal surfaces and containers derived from one seed colour.
Clarity · in the spirit of the HIG: grouped white cards, crisp type, translucent bars only.
Blueprint · wildcard: the car as a technical drawing, with white linework on a drafting grid.
Light packs (Slab, Paper, Soft, Clay, Clarity) automatically use darker status-text tones (#1f7a4b / #8a6110 / #c0283f); filled telltales are unchanged. Soft is the weakest for driving because neumorphic edges fade in sunlight, so treat it as phone and passenger first.
R2Round 2 · new languages
Glass, made more transparent: cards drop from 62 % to 30 % opacity with a 22 px blur.
Air: floating frosted cards (10 %, 30 px blur), a light rim and float shadow, and tall thin condensed numerals. Based on your reference.
Ledger: fintech principles (monochrome surfaces, pill-everything, no shadows, one scarce cobalt). It is an original theme, not a brand copy, and avoids proprietary fonts and brand hexes.
Tide: chart-grade precision and nautical teal. Inspired by the 2026 Apple Design Award winner for Visuals & Graphics (a tide-charts app).
Lunar: an ink-violet night sky with silver numerals. Inspired by the 2026 Interaction winner (a moon-phase tracker).
Tactile: physical bevelled keys and segmented dials. Inspired by the 2026 Visuals finalists' skeuomorphic, tactile camera UI.
