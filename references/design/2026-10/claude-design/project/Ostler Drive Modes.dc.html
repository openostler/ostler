OSTLER · DRIVE MODES · NIGHT
Seven Drive modes, the switcher, passenger view and settings
Every Moving screen is built only from the allowed templates (tiles, map, media, alert card, short list, call) and fits on one screen. The strip is always shown, and the Drive-mode switcher chip sits on the driver side (right), just before the clock. Panels go on the passenger side (left). No glow or blur anywhere.
Compact strips — on HU-5 and phone, chip labels shorten (TD5, 2, the Ghost icon) so every chip still fits at 24 px. On phone the strip wraps to two rows.
Maps — real OpenFreeMap dark tiles captured as images. The route ahead is cyan, the trail behind uses the speed ramp. Ride markers are plain while Moving, and the coarse buddy is a soft ±3 km disc.
Tweaks — Faults and Ghost/Visible apply to every strip, status line and alert.
