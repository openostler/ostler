Open questions: proposals
C-Q1Unknown motion vs movingSame rules as moving; the strip shows an outlined “Motion unknown” with a help icon instead of the lock (C-04).
C-Q2Focus order across tilesDriver-side column first, top to bottom, then passenger side, then the strip (C-27).
C-Q3Soft keys when the page has noneHide the row on 5in (overlay only, no shift). On 7in and up keep the row with the owner tag and empty slots, so tiles never jump.
C-Q4Which tile owns the soft keysAn owner tag at the driver end of the row with the page icon and name in the focus-ring colour, matching the ring on the tile.
C-Q5Warning band on a portrait phoneAcross the bottom (C-18). A side band is too narrow for 24 px text.
C-Q6Tapping a caution pillOpens the alerts list; each row opens its page beside, or in place if it’s already on screen.
C-Q7Is “+n” enough?Yes, with the alerts list one tap away from the pill and on long Select.
C-Q8Long Select with no warningOpens the active alerts list, or says “No alerts” in the strip for 2 s.
C-Q9Warnings while another app is in frontOstler Controls shows the band over that app; cautions only chime and light its strip.
C-Q10Refusal reason for a wheel action off screenA text-only toast at the top of the screen for 4 s, e.g. “Clear faults: not while moving”.
