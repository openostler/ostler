Open questions: proposals
B-Q1Vehicle chip with no gateway or no carHide it, and let the connection chip say “No gateway”. The strip shifts once, at first connect, which is acceptable.
B-Q2Open beside: edit or page action?A page action: allowed while moving when it replaces a tile (see A-Q1). Adding a tile waits until parked.
B-Q3Two layouts on the same triggerBlock it: picking a taken trigger asks “Move it from Commute?”.
B-Q4Automatic switch while movingJust the strip name changing, plus a text-only toast with Undo for 4 s. No sound.
B-Q5Merged chip: car or status firstStatus word whenever not connected; the car name only when connected (B-10).
B-Q6Switcher cap while movingSix rows, in the user’s order from Manage layouts.
B-Q7Badging saved iconsA tiles badge for layouts and a square badge for single pages, bottom right (B-33).
