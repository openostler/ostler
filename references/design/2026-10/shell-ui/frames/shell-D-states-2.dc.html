Where themes touch the shell (D8)Frame surface, corner shape and edge; label type and style; strip style (bar, floating, line); chip and button shapes; divider handle look; wallpaper. Themes cannot change size thresholds, touch targets, the 24 px moving floor, caution and warning colours, the focus ring’s visibility, or safety icons.
What the shell does not do (D9)No settings screens (it opens the gateway’s shared settings), no Car page, no car logic, no connection fixing (it hands off to the Gateway), no launcher, no notifications of its own.
Open questions: proposals
D-Q1How long a dismissed banner stays awayUntil its condition changes. If the same condition returns after clearing, the banner comes back.
D-Q2Banner on 480 px-high screensOverlay the top of the tiles (D-10), so no tile drops a class.
D-Q3Banner when the car starts movingHide at once; the chip already carries the state.
D-Q4“Last seen” for older dataTime today, “Yesterday 18:10”, then a date: “2 Oct 18:10”. No “3 h ago”, which goes stale itself.
D-Q5“Get more apps” in the layout switcherKeep it in the apps menu when only the Gateway is installed (B-29), not in the layout switcher.
D-Q6Third-party marker at BadgeA 14 px puzzle icon in the corner (D-22); the full text is in the label menu.
D-Q7Where a stale tile shows “Last seen”In the label (consistent across pages); the page dims its values.
