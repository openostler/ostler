Open questions: proposals
A-Q1Open beside and Close while movingOpen beside stays one tap while moving when it adds no new tile (it replaces the passenger tile, with Undo), because it is navigation. Close stays parked-only.
A-Q2Soft-key row and the height budget on 5inOn 5in the soft-key row overlays the bottom 48 px of the focused tile and only appears for 4 s after a wheel key or a tap. The tile keeps its class. On 7in and larger it counts against the tile area.
A-Q3Label row at Compact while movingYes: at Compact while moving the label becomes the page icon and chevron (A-02 Moving, C-02). The full name stays in the menu and for screen readers.
A-Q4Driver side in portraitThe main tile goes at the top (eyes reach it first). The driver side still decides soft-key order and which side chips sit on in landscape.
A-Q5Too many chipsThe last chip becomes “+n” and opens a list sheet. Nothing is dropped silently.
A-Q6“Show sizes”A designer and debug aid, off by default. It sits under About in the gateway’s shared settings and never shows while moving.
A-Q7UnlinkingKeep a visible link mark. Tapping it offers Unlink. Links also end when either tile is swapped or closed.
