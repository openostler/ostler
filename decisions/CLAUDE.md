- `adr-0025-reuse-and-licences-pragmatic.md` — amends ADR-0019 (c): reimplement anything in our own words; never copy descriptive text; GPL-3 taken whole only into marked GPL-3 modules or packs; GPL-2.0-only, non-commercial and dealer material stay out.

Amended: PLCA coordinator and broker on the node. Amended by ADR-0039: USB-NCM node-to-Brain link beside T1S.
- `adr-0027-ip-everywhere-ecosystem-architecture.md` — builds on ADR-0026: IP everywhere on an automotive-Ethernet backbone (T1S modules, Ethernet/PoE cameras, 100BASE-T1 only for our own cameras, Wi-Fi/USB displays); the Pi routes; MQTT 5 with VSS topics; mDNS/DNS-SD; NTP; mTLS (envelope dropped, v1.1); the module contract and DevicePack adapters; car buses at the edge; CAN/wake fallback; Matter via a bridge only. Superseded in part by ADR-0032 and ADR-0033. Amended by ADR-0039: node link segment (USB-NCM), `tap/` and `lab/` topics.
- `adr-0028-base-hardware-connectivity-and-remote-access.md` — the base is the Pi plus an always-on ESP32 "buddy" (wake, buses, small parked MQTT broker bridged to the Pi); the guardian is an add-on; uplinks (car/head-unit Wi-Fi, hotspot, any USB dongle, OpenWrt gateway, Starlink) with Auto failover and metering; remote access LAN → Tailscale → Ostler Cloud → HA Cloud; Improv provisioning; IANA `_ostler-mod._tcp`. - `adr-0032-one-node-optional-brain.md` — one ESP32 node (now Ostler Diagnostics, ADR-0039; was "Ostler Lite"; optional 4G, works offline with a phone) with an optional Pi brain (now Ostler Brain; was Hub). Amended by ADR-0039 (USB-NCM product link, UART dev-only, u-blox on the node) and ADR-0040 (wake and shutdown extended). - `adr-0043-gps-and-logs-in-shared-trips.md` — ACCEPTED (owner, 2026-10-07, "approve all", DMD round): the ADR that ADR-0009 asks for. ## Editing rules

- Never edit an accepted ADR's decision. Supersede it with a new ADR and set the old
  one's frontmatter `status: superseded`.
- Name new ADRs `adr-NNNN-kebab-title.md`, with the next number (never reused).
- New ADRs (from ADR-0016) follow MADR: add **Decision drivers** and **Confirmation** sections
  (ADR-0017).
- Rebuild INDEX.md after adding an ADR.
