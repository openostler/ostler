# Trademarks

**"Ostler"** and **"OpenOstler"** are trademarks of the project maintainer (James Wright),
and so are the Ostler logos. A UK registration is pending.

The code licence (AGPL-3.0-or-later) and the data licence (CC BY-SA 4.0) cover the code and
the data. **They do not grant any right to use the names or logos** (see ADR-0012 and
ADR-0014).

## Allowed without asking

- **Saying what your product works with**, e.g. "works with Ostler", "an Ostler vehicle
  pack for …", "fork of OpenOstler". The name must not suggest that the project made your
  product or endorses it.
- **Mentioning the project** in articles, talks, reviews and tutorials.
- **Running unmodified official releases** under their own name.

## Needs permission

- **Distributing a modified version, hardware or a hosted service under the Ostler or
  OpenOstler name**, or under a confusingly similar one. Forks must rename. Keep the
  copyright and licence notices; remove the marks.
- **Using the names or logos in your own product or company name, domain, or social
  handle.**
- **Selling merchandise.**

Ask through the repository's issues. Ostler Cloud and official Ostler hardware are offered
only by the maintainer.
